# Power BI Developer Accelerator — 4-Part Training Package

## Package contents
1. `01_Trainer_Guide.pdf` — day-by-day trainer curriculum with theory, labs, assignments, answer guidance and certification rubric.
2. `02_Learner_Workbook.pdf` — learner-facing worksheets, checklists, design/validation notes and reflection pages.
3. `03_Lab_Datasets/` — synthetic Retail, Healthcare, Supply Chain and Sales datasets plus legacy migration lab notes.
4. `05_Claude_Prompt_Library.md` — reusable prompts for requirements, DAX, Power Query, SQL, UX, testing and legacy migration.
5. `06_Quiz_Answer_Key.pdf` — 50 daily quiz questions with expected answers.
6. `07_Capstone_Specifications.pdf` — three customer-style capstones.
7. `08_Capstone_Assessment.xlsx` — scoring rubric, checklist and quiz tracker.

## Delivery
- 10 days
- 6 hours/day
- 2 hours guided theory
- 4 hours hands-on
- 60 total hours

## Certification
70% overall is required, with no critical failure in model correctness, KPI validation or capstone integrity.
